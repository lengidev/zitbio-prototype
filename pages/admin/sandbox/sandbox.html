Open Ecosystem Scenarios

Coupled model demonstration

The ecological model is demonstrated in Ecosystem Scenarios, where a what-if changes a scenario count without changing any official data.

What the model demonstrates

A population change is not isolated. More zebra increases relative forage demand; the same forage supports puku and waterbuck; grazing and trampling pressure can affect water and soil. Each relationship carries its stated mechanism and evidence tier.

Open Ecosystem Scenarios to change a species count and follow the connected pathways. The result shows exact arithmetic, qualitative effects, and the measurements still needed—without saving the scenario.

Registered baselines, approved observations, regional evidence, and estimates remain visibly distinguishable.
