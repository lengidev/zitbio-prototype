Open Ecological State

Coupled model demonstration

The ecological model is now demonstrated in Ecological State, where a what-if changes a recorded fauna count without changing any official data.

What the model demonstrates

A population change is not isolated. More zebra increases daily forage demand; the same forage supports puku and waterbuck; grazing and trampling pressure can affect water and soil. Each relationship carries its stated mechanism and provenance.

Open Ecological State, choose a zone with current approved census data, and change a standing fauna count. The result recalculates forage duration, demand, zebra-equivalents, the capacity verdict, and all documented coupled effects—without saving the scenario.

Prototype values are visibly labelled. Measured values and recorded counts always remain distinct from hypothetical inputs.
